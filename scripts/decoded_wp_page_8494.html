Component 1 • Coursework (20%) • 60 Marks Externally Assessed Coursework

Recording & Mixing

Produce a full commercial cover recording selected from an annually updated list of designated artists provided by Edexcel. Master acoustic capture, microphone polar patterns, stereo arrays (XY, spaced pair), preamp gain staging, and multi-track balance.

AS vs A-Level: Full A-Level requires acoustic drums and percussion, whereas AS specifies no drums. Completing the AS brief first provides a rock-solid stepping stone for Year 12 students.

Component 2 • Coursework (20%) • 60 Marks Creative DAW Composition

Sequencing, Sampling, Synthesis & Composition

Undertake an original 3-minute composition on a Digital Audio Workstation (Logic Pro X or Ableton) responding to official Edexcel briefs. You will incorporate two mandatory samples, demonstrating synthesis sound design, creative sampling manipulation, automation, and structured sonic development.

Key Advice: Starting with the AS brief helps refine creative pacing, MIDI sequencing, and arrangement before tackling the full A-Level portfolio.

Component 3 • Written Exam (25%) • 1h 30m Written Listening Paper

Listening & Analysing (Music Technology in Context)

Explore the historical evolution of music technology and its profound impact on musical creativity. Study early acoustic capture, multi-track magnetic tape, electric guitar innovation, synthesizer pioneers, digital sampling, and modern production fingerprints.

Exam Preparation: Master critical listening with audio-based questions, presentations, video breakdowns, and our comprehensive 500+ term Music Technology Dictionary.

Component 4 • Practical Exam (35%) • 2h 15m DAW Examination

Producing & Analysing (Production Techniques & Exam)

Hone the practical engineering skills needed to edit, fix, and mix under timed examination conditions. Tutorials cover surgical parametric EQ, compression ratios and attack/release timing, creative distortion, delay/reverb spatial routing, and automation.

Practical Testing: Work through real sample exam stems and model mark schemes to guarantee precision and speed on exam day.
