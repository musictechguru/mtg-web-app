Balanced Cable ↗ Cables & Connections

A cable containing three conductors: Hot (+), Cold (- inverted phase), and Ground shield. At the receiving differential amplifier, the cold signal is flipped and added to hot, doubling signal strength and completely cancelling induced electromagnetic hum (common-mode rejection).
