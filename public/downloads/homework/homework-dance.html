🔍 Task 2: The 'Big 4' Specification Fingerprints

Memorize these core specification criteria across Equipment, Capture, Processing, and Soundstage:

1. Equipment & Instruments

Akai S1000/S3000XL hardware samplers, E-mu SP-1200, Roland JD-800, Clavia Nord Lead, Access Virus virtual analog synth, Alesis 3630 compressor, Mackie 8-bus console, early DAWs (Cubase, Logic Audio).

2. Audio Capture & Micing

Digital audio sampling directly from vinyl records. Direct-in digital recording via S/PDIF and ADAT optical connections into early computer audio interfaces.

3. Processing & Effects

Aggressive sidechain pumping compression (ducking bass/chords against the four-on-the-floor kick). Timestretching algorithm artifacts (metallic chorus/granular echoes). Bitcrushing and distortion plugins.

4. Soundstage & Balance

Massive dynamic contrast between breakdown and drop. Narrow mono build-ups that suddenly explode into ultra-wide stereo choruses with side-panned synthesizer arpeggios and stereo delay throws.

📝 Task 3: Section A Exam Practice Questions (10 Marks)

Answer the following Edexcel exam questions in full technical sentences using professional studio terminology:

Question 1 [3 Marks]

Explain the mechanics of 'sidechain compression' and how Daft Punk utilized it on tracks like 'One More Time'.

Write your answer here (or complete on paper)...
Examiner Guidance: The compressor's detector circuit is fed by an external key input (the kick drum) instead of the main audio signal, automatically pulling down (ducking) the volume of the synth/bass on every kick hit, creating a rhythmic pumping sensation.
Question 2 [3 Marks]

Describe the sonic artifacts produced by early 1990s hardware samplers (e.g. Akai S950) when performing extreme time-stretching.

Write your answer here (or complete on paper)...
Examiner Guidance: Granular smearing, metallic ringing, phase cancellation, stuttered transient repetitions, and a hollow comb-filtered frequency texture.
Question 3 [1 Marks]

State the synthesizer used to play the iconic pizzicato build-up riff on Faithless's 'Insomnia'.

Write your answer here (or complete on paper)...
Examiner Guidance: Roland JD-800 (or Roland JV-1080 / JD-990 Pizzagogo patch).
Question 4 [1 Marks]

Identify the breakbeat sampled from The Winstons' 'Amen, Brother' that underpins jungle and breakbeat hardcore.

Write your answer here (or complete on paper)...
Examiner Guidance: The 'Amen Break'.
✍️ Task 4: Section B Extended 12-Mark Comparative Essay
Official 12-Mark Prompt

Evaluate the sampling techniques and signal processing of The Prodigy's 'Firestarter' (1996) compared to Daft Punk's 'Da Funk' (1995). Examine: • Hardware Sampler Architecture & Loop Chopping • Compression as an Artistic Effect (Sidechain/Pumping) • Synthesis Distortion & Transient Attack.

Indicative Examiner Mark Scheme Content:

Contrast Liam Howlett's Akai S3200 sample chopping of live guitar/drum loops, aggressive Mackie preamp distortion, and industrial punk breakbeats against Daft Punk's Alesis 3630 sidechain compression pumping, Mutator analog filter modulation, and filtered electro funk samples.
