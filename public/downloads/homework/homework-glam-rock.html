🔍 Task 2: The 'Big 4' Specification Fingerprints

Memorize these core specification criteria across Equipment, Capture, Processing, and Soundstage:

1. Equipment & Instruments

Gibson Les Paul, Gibson SG, custom 'Deacy' germanium transistor amplifier, Ludwig & Premier drum kits, acoustic 12-string guitars, Mellotron, upright acoustic pianos (Trident's C. Bechstein piano).

2. Audio Capture & Micing

16-track and 24-track tape machines. Intimate close-miking on drums with dampened skins (tea towels or wallets on snare). Multi-layered vocal overdubs using Neumann U67/U87 condensers.

3. Processing & Effects

Intense track-to-track tape bouncing causing tape compression and gentle saturation. Flanging and phaser pedals (MXR Phase 90). Plate reverberation (EMT 140). Heavy bus compression on rhythm section.

4. Soundstage & Balance

Widescreen stereo mixing. Rhythms centered; layered backing vocal harmonies panned hard left and hard right to create massive vocal wall; harmonized lead guitars panned symmetrically.

📝 Task 3: Section A Exam Practice Questions (10 Marks)

Answer the following Edexcel exam questions in full technical sentences using professional studio terminology:

Question 1 [3 Marks]

Explain the process of tape reduction bouncing used by Roy Thomas Baker when producing Queen's 'Bohemian Rhapsody'.

Write your answer here (or complete on paper)...
Examiner Guidance: Recording multiple vocal tracks onto 24-track tape, then submixing them down to a stereo pair on another machine to free up tracks, repeated until tape became virtually transparent from oxide wear.
Question 2 [1 Marks]

Identify the acoustic piano located at Trident Studios that defined the sound of Elton John, David Bowie, and Queen recordings.

Write your answer here (or complete on paper)...
Examiner Guidance: 100-year-old C. Bechstein grand piano, celebrated for its bright, percussive cut in dense rock mixes.
Question 3 [3 Marks]

Describe how engineers in early 1970s glam rock achieved a tight, dry, punchy snare drum sound.

Write your answer here (or complete on paper)...
Examiner Guidance: Dampening snare head with tape, wallets, or tea towels; deadening studio room acoustic with baffles; close-miking with Shure SM57; applying fast gate/compression.
Question 4 [3 Marks]

State the audio effect used extensively on Brian May's guitar harmonies in 'Killer Queen'.

Write your answer here (or complete on paper)...
Examiner Guidance: Tape flanging, subtle phaser modulation, and dynamic ADT double tracking.
