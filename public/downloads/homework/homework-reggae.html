🔍 Task 2: The 'Big 4' Specification Fingerprints

Memorize these core specification criteria across Equipment, Capture, Processing, and Soundstage:

1. Equipment & Instruments

Fender Jazz Bass (neck pickup with tone control rolled off), flatwound bass strings, Fender Stratocaster/Telecaster (playing muted off-beat skank), Hammond B3 organ, Hohner Clavinet, acoustic drums with de-tuned snare (timbales).

2. Audio Capture & Micing

MCI and Soundcraft consoles. Direct Injection (DI) for bass guitar fundamental warmth. Close-miking snare and hi-hat. Deadened tracking rooms with heavy baffling.

3. Processing & Effects

Dub mixing desk manipulation: driving auxiliary sends in real time. Roland Space Echo RE-201 (feedback/repeats pushed to oscillation). Fisher and Grampian spring reverb tanks physically kicked or slapped to create thunderous explosive crashes.

4. Soundstage & Balance

Massive frequency contrast: heavy low-end sub-bass (40–100Hz) and bright hi-hat/percussion sizzle with midrange cleared for vocal drops. Spatial delays ping-ponged across stereo field; sudden mutes of bass and drums dropping into cavernous reverb.

📝 Task 3: Section A Exam Practice Questions (10 Marks)

Answer the following Edexcel exam questions in full technical sentences using professional studio terminology:

Question 1 [2 Marks]

Describe the mechanical action used by King Tubby and Lee Perry to create the iconic thunder-clap sound effect on dub reggae records.

Write your answer here (or complete on paper)...
Examiner Guidance: Physically kicking, hitting, or slapping the metal chassis of a spring reverb tank, causing the suspended springs to violently crash together.
Question 2 [3 Marks]

Explain how reggae bass players achieve their deep, rounded low-end tone without finger noise or excessive upper-mid clatter.

Write your answer here (or complete on paper)...
Examiner Guidance: Fender Jazz/Precision bass with flatwound strings, playing with thumb near the neck, tone knob rolled 100% off, recorded through a tube DI box with low-pass filtering.
Question 3 [3 Marks]

State the key hardware delay unit deployed in 1970s Kingston dub studios, and name two parameters manipulated in real time.

Write your answer here (or complete on paper)...
Examiner Guidance: Roland Space Echo RE-201. Parameters: Repeat Rate (delay time), Intensity (feedback/regeneration), and Echo Volume/Wet Level.
Question 4 [2 Marks]

Identify the two off-beat keyboard patterns characteristic of classic roots reggae rhythm sections.

Write your answer here (or complete on paper)...
Examiner Guidance: The piano/guitar 'skank' on beats 2 and 4, and the Hammond organ 'bubble' (shuffle groove doubling the 16th-note rhythm).
