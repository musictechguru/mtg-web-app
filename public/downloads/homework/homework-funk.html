🔍 Task 2: The 'Big 4' Specification Fingerprints

Memorize these core specification criteria across Equipment, Capture, Processing, and Soundstage:

1. Equipment & Instruments

Fender Jazz & Precision Bass, Minimoog Model D analog synthesizer, Fender Stratocaster (middle/bridge pickup), Hohner Clavinet D6, Mu-Tron III envelope filter, MXR Phase 90, Ludwig drums, Maestro Rhythm King drum machine.

2. Audio Capture & Micing

Tight close-micing. Snare miked top and bottom (SM57) with phase reversed on bottom mic. Close-miking brass horns with Neumann U87s and RCA ribbons. Bass guitar double-tracked or DI blended with mic on Ampeg SVT amp.

3. Processing & Effects

Envelope follower filters (auto-wah), optical compression (Teletronix LA-2A) on vocals, fast VCA compression (dbx 160) on kick and slap bass to clamp down percussive peaks, subtle tape saturation.

4. Soundstage & Balance

Hyper-focused percussive stereo soundstage. Kick drum and bass guitar locked in mono center; Clavinet and rhythm guitar panned opposite each other (9 o'clock and 3 o'clock); wide stereo horn section flourishes.

📝 Task 3: Section A Exam Practice Questions (10 Marks)

Answer the following Edexcel exam questions in full technical sentences using professional studio terminology:

Question 1 [1 Marks]

State the synthesizer model used by Bernie Worrell to lay down the iconic bassline of Parliament's 'Flash Light' (1977).

Write your answer here (or complete on paper)...
Examiner Guidance: Minimoog (Minimoog Model D analog synthesizer).
Question 2 [3 Marks]

Explain the technical operation of the Mu-Tron III envelope filter pedal used on funk bass and clavinet tracks.

Write your answer here (or complete on paper)...
Examiner Guidance: An envelope follower circuit detects the incoming pick/string audio amplitude and converts it into a control voltage (CV) that sweeps the filter cutoff frequency proportionally to playing dynamics.
Question 3 [3 Marks]

Describe the drum production technique used on 1970s funk snare drums to achieve maximum articulation of ghost notes.

Write your answer here (or complete on paper)...
Examiner Guidance: Dual micing: top mic (Shure SM57) capturing stick attack transient; bottom mic capturing snare wire sizzle; phase polarity inverted on bottom mic to avoid phase cancellation.
Question 4 [2 Marks]

Identify the historical significance of Sly & The Family Stone's 'Family Affair' (1971) regarding drum technology.

Write your answer here (or complete on paper)...
Examiner Guidance: One of the earliest commercial pop hit records to utilize an electronic rhythm unit (Maestro Rhythm King MRK-2 drum machine) instead of a live drummer.

Official 12-Mark Prompt

Indicative Examiner Mark Scheme Content:

Contrast James Brown's live, unedited acoustic single-take ensemble performance featuring Bootsy's organic fingerstyle bass and dry funk drums against George Clinton and Bernie Worrell's multi-layered Minimoog synth bass, synthesized laser effects, and heavily processed 24-track studio wizardry.
