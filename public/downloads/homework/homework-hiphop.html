🔍 Task 2: The 'Big 4' Specification Fingerprints

Memorize these core specification criteria across Equipment, Capture, Processing, and Soundstage:

1. Equipment & Instruments

E-mu SP-1200 (12-bit, 26.04kHz sampling), Akai MPC60 / MPC3000 (Roger Linn swing algorithms), Roland TR-808, Technics SL-1200 turntables, modern DAWs (FL Studio, Pro Tools).

2. Audio Capture & Micing

High-end vocal capture in vocal booths: Sony C-800G tube microphone (with built-in Peltier cooling system), Neumann U87, Avalon VT-737sp tube channel strip. Turntable needle scratching miked directly.

3. Processing & Effects

12-bit ring modulation sample artifacts, heavy sub-bass saturation (distorting 808 kick tails), pitch-shifting vocals and samples, extreme digital vocal pitch correction (Auto-Tune) as creative stylistic effect.

4. Soundstage & Balance

Monstrous low-frequency mono focus: sub-bass 808 tuned specifically to the song key (30–60Hz) paired with a tight, knocking kick transient (80–120Hz); vocal centered and forward; trap hi-hats ticking across stereo field.

📝 Task 3: Section A Exam Practice Questions (10 Marks)

Answer the following Edexcel exam questions in full technical sentences using professional studio terminology:

Question 1 [3 Marks]

Explain why hip-hop producers prized the E-mu SP-1200 sampler for drum and loop programming in the late 1980s.

Write your answer here (or complete on paper)...
Examiner Guidance: 12-bit resolution and 26.04kHz sample rate imparted a warm, gritty harmonic crunch; pitch-shifting down samples cut at 45 RPM introduced distinctive aliasing artifacts and extended available sample time.
Question 2 [2 Marks]

Describe the synthesizer patch technique used by Dr. Dre to create the signature G-Funk high-register lead sound.

Write your answer here (or complete on paper)...
Examiner Guidance: A monophonic analog synthesizer (Minimoog) set to high octave with long portamento (glide) between notes and subtle pitch vibrato.
Question 3 [2 Marks]

State the standard microphone and preamp chain used on modern commercial hip-hop vocals for a bright, pristine top end.

Write your answer here (or complete on paper)...
Examiner Guidance: Sony C-800G tube condenser microphone paired with an Avalon VT-737sp tube preamp/compressor.
Question 4 [3 Marks]

Explain how modern trap producers tune an 808 kick drum to prevent harmonic clashes with the musical arrangement.

Write your answer here (or complete on paper)...
Examiner Guidance: They determine the song's root key and pitch-shift the 808 sample in the DAW sampler so its fundamental sub-bass frequency matches the bass note of the chord progression.
✍️ Task 4: Section B Extended 12-Mark Comparative Essay
Official 12-Mark Prompt

Evaluate the evolution of sampling and rhythm production from late-1980s Boom Bap (Public Enemy, 1989) to modern Trap production (Kendrick Lamar / Travis Scott, 2017+). In your answer, address: • Sampling Workstations & Digital Resolution • Drum Synthesis: Boom Bap Breaks vs. 808 Sub-Bass & Hi-Hat Rolls • Vocal Engineering & Spatial Panning.

Indicative Examiner Mark Scheme Content:

Contrast The Bomb Squad's E-mu SP-1200 sample collisions, vinyl scratching, and dense funk loops against modern Trap's FL Studio piano roll sequencing, synthesized 808 sub-bass gliding, rapid 32nd-note triplet hi-hat rolls, and Auto-Tuned vocal treatments.
