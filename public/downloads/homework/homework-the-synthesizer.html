🔍 Task 2: The 'Big 4' Specification Fingerprints

Memorize these core specification criteria across Equipment, Capture, Processing, and Soundstage:

1. Equipment & Instruments

Moog Modular 55, Minimoog Model D, Sequential Prophet-5, Roland Jupiter-8, Yamaha DX7, Roland TB-303, Korg MS-20, Fairlight CMI.

2. Audio Capture & Micing

Direct line injection (DI) into console preamps to maintain pure harmonic frequency response and eliminate microphone acoustic coloration.

3. Processing & Effects

Low-pass resonant filtering (VCF), 4-stage ADSR envelope shaping of amplitude and filter cutoff, sub-audible LFO modulation (vibrato, tremolo, filter sweeps), analog chorus.

4. Soundstage & Balance

Stereo panning of multi-tracked oscillators; stereo chorus widening polyphonic pads; mono center placement for sub-bass and kick drums.

📝 Task 3: Section A Exam Practice Questions (10 Marks)

Answer the following Edexcel exam questions in full technical sentences using professional studio terminology:

Question 1 [3 Marks]

Explain the difference between a Sawtooth waveform and a Square waveform in terms of harmonic content.

Write your answer here (or complete on paper)...
Examiner Guidance: Sawtooth contains ALL integer harmonics (1f, 2f, 3f, 4f...) with amplitude inversely proportional to harmonic number (1/n); Square wave contains ONLY ODD harmonics (1f, 3f, 5f, 7f...) with amplitude 1/n.
Question 2 [3 Marks]

Describe the function of the Resonance control on a 24dB/octave Low-Pass Filter.

Write your answer here (or complete on paper)...
Examiner Guidance: Feedback loop that emphasizes and boosts frequencies right at the filter cutoff point; at high settings, it goes into self-oscillation producing a pure sine wave.
Question 3 [2 Marks]

State the year MIDI 1.0 was standardized, and name the connector used.

Write your answer here (or complete on paper)...
Examiner Guidance: 1983; 5-pin DIN connector cable (operating at 31.25 kbaud serial transfer).
Question 4 [2 Marks]

Identify the two envelopes present on a standard subtractive synthesizer architecture and what each controls.

Write your answer here (or complete on paper)...
Examiner Guidance: Filter Envelope (controls VCF cutoff sweep over time) and Amplifier Envelope (controls VCA volume level over time).
