🔍 Task 2: The 'Big 4' Specification Fingerprints

Memorize these core specification criteria across Equipment, Capture, Processing, and Soundstage:

1. Equipment & Instruments

Hollow and semi-hollow electric guitars, small Fender Champ or Gibson valve combos pushed into overdrive, Green Bullet harmonica mic, upright bass transitioning to Fender Precision Bass, acoustic drums.

2. Audio Capture & Micing

Shure 55 'Elvis mic' or RCA ribbons. Close miking harmonica through amplifier. Foot stomping boards miked with dynamic mics. Room bleed embraced as part of live energy.

3. Processing & Effects

Pure tube harmonic distortion (preamp and output tube saturation). Hallway or concrete basement acoustic echo chambers. Tape compression from hot tracking onto early magnetic tape.

4. Soundstage & Balance

Early recordings strictly mono. Late 1960s recordings exhibit wide stereo panning (guitar hard left, rhythm section center/right, strings wide stereo).

📝 Task 3: Section A Exam Practice Questions (10 Marks)

Answer the following Edexcel exam questions in full technical sentences using professional studio terminology:

Question 1 [2 Marks]

Describe how harmonica players on Chess Records electric blues sessions achieved their gritty, distorted tone.

Write your answer here (or complete on paper)...
Examiner Guidance: High-impedance dynamic bullet mic cupped tightly against harmonica, overloading input stage of small valve guitar combo.
Question 2 [3 Marks]

Explain the mechanical difference between direct-to-disc recording (1930s) and magnetic tape recording (1950s).

Write your answer here (or complete on paper)...
Examiner Guidance: Disc: stylus cuts groove directly into acetate/lacquer in real time without editing; Tape: audio magnetized onto iron-oxide coated tape allowing splicing, multiple takes, and delay loops.
Question 3 [2 Marks]

State one acoustic consequence of Muddy Waters' band recording simultaneously around two microphones at 2120 S. Michigan Avenue.

Write your answer here (or complete on paper)...
Examiner Guidance: Extensive room spill and acoustic bleed between guitar amp, drums, and upright bass; balance fixed at source.
Question 4 [3 Marks]

Identify three sonic hallmarks of B.B. King's guitar production on 'The Thrill Is Gone'.

Write your answer here (or complete on paper)...
Examiner Guidance: Clean-to-mildly saturated tube tone, warm mid-range EQ, smooth dynamic compression, lush stereo reverb tail.
