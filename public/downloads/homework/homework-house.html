🔍 Task 2: The 'Big 4' Specification Fingerprints

Memorize these core specification criteria across Equipment, Capture, Processing, and Soundstage:

1. Equipment & Instruments

Roland TR-909 (analog kick/snare with 6-bit sampled cymbals), Roland TR-808 (sub-bass boom kick), Roland TB-303 (diode ladder filter), Akai S900/S950 samplers, Yamaha DX100 (LatelyBass patch), Korg M1 (Organ 2 patch).

2. Audio Capture & Micing

Direct line input to budget mixers (Seck, Studiomaster, Mackie, Boss). Direct to cassette or reel-to-reel master. Minimal microphone usage except for soulful gospel-style vocal overdubs.

3. Processing & Effects

Overdriving channel preamps on budget analog mixers to create aggressive crunch. Extreme filter resonance self-oscillation on the TB-303. Budget Alesis Microverb / Midiverb digital reverbs.

4. Soundstage & Balance

Monolithic dancefloor mix. TR-909 kick drum, TB-303 bassline, and lead vocals strictly centered for club subwoofers; 909 open/closed hi-hats slightly panned; vocal stabs and synth chords panned wide.

📝 Task 3: Section A Exam Practice Questions (10 Marks)

Answer the following Edexcel exam questions in full technical sentences using professional studio terminology:

Question 1 [3 Marks]

Identify the five front-panel rotary controls manipulated live by DJ Pierre on the Roland TB-303 to create 'Acid Tracks'.

Write your answer here (or complete on paper)...
Examiner Guidance: Tuning, Cutoff Frequency, Resonance, Envelope Mod (or Decay), and Accent (plus Slide switches).
Question 2 [3 Marks]

Explain the architectural hybrid nature of the Roland TR-909 drum machine regarding its sound generation.

Write your answer here (or complete on paper)...
Examiner Guidance: Analog synthesizer circuits generate the kick drum, snare, toms, and rimshot; 6-bit digital PCM samples are used for the hi-hats, ride, and crash cymbals.
Question 3 [2 Marks]

State the synthesizer model and patch number responsible for the ubiquitous 1990s house organ bassline (e.g. Robin S).

Write your answer here (or complete on paper)...
Examiner Guidance: Korg M1 synthesizer workstation, patch: 'Organ 2'.
Question 4 [2 Marks]

Describe how house producers utilized the 16th-note swing parameter on Roland drum machines to create a human groove.

Write your answer here (or complete on paper)...
Examiner Guidance: Shifting every even sixteenth note slightly later in time (typically 54% to 62% swing) to transform rigid mechanical timing into a rolling dance shuffle.
✍️ Task 4: Section B Extended 12-Mark Comparative Essay
Official 12-Mark Prompt

Evaluate the production and technology of Marshall Jefferson's 'Move Your Body' (1986) compared to Phuture's 'Acid Tracks' (1987). Focus on: • Drum Machine Architecture & Grooves • Harmonic Instrumentation vs. Monophonic Filter Modulation • Budget Recording Environments & Club Impact.

Indicative Examiner Mark Scheme Content:

Contrast Jefferson's emotive gospel-rooted acoustic piano and multi-voiced TR-909 arrangement against Phuture's hypnotic, single-instrument TB-303 acid squelch, minimalist percussion, and real-time filter sweep manipulation.
