🔍 Task 2: The 'Big 4' Specification Fingerprints

Memorize these core specification criteria across Equipment, Capture, Processing, and Soundstage:

1. Equipment & Instruments

Roland Jupiter-8, Sequential Circuits Prophet-5, Yamaha DX7 (FM synthesis), Roland TB-303, LinnDrum / Oberheim DMX / Roland TR-808 drum machines, Fairlight CMI sampler, MIDI 1.0 protocol (1983+).

2. Audio Capture & Micing

Almost entirely direct line-level signals (synths and drum machines recorded DI). Vocals recorded in pristine isolation using Neumann U87, Sony C-800G, or Shure SM7B through high-end outboard compressors.

3. Processing & Effects

Non-linear and gated digital reverb (AMS RMX16 NonLin 2, Lexicon 224/480L), digital delays (AMS DMX 15-80S), aggressive SSL channel gate on snare, early pitch harmonizers (Eventide H910/H949).

4. Soundstage & Balance

Ultra-wide, pristine digital stereo field. Centered punching digital kick, gated reverb snare, and quantized synth bass; polyphonic synth pads and sampled orchestral brass panned wide with stereo chorus.

📝 Task 3: Section A Exam Practice Questions (10 Marks)

Answer the following Edexcel exam questions in full technical sentences using professional studio terminology:

Question 1 [1 Marks]

State the key specification standard introduced in 1983 that allowed synthesizers and drum machines from different manufacturers to communicate.

Write your answer here (or complete on paper)...
Examiner Guidance: MIDI (Musical Instrument Digital Interface 1.0).
Question 2 [3 Marks]

Explain the difference between Subtractive Synthesis (e.g. Roland Jupiter-8) and Frequency Modulation (FM) Synthesis (e.g. Yamaha DX7).

Write your answer here (or complete on paper)...
Examiner Guidance: Subtractive: filters out harmonics from rich waveforms (sawtooth/square) with a resonant VCF; FM: modulates frequency of a sine carrier wave with audio-rate modulator operators without using traditional filters.
Question 3 [3 Marks]

Describe the gated reverb effect deployed on 1980s synth-pop snare drums and name the iconic processor used.

Write your answer here (or complete on paper)...
Examiner Guidance: A large hall or room reverb tail suddenly cut off abruptly by a noise gate before it can naturally decay. Processor: AMS RMX16 (NonLin program) or Lexicon 224.
Question 4 [1 Marks]

Identify the digital sampling workstation used by Tears for Fears and Pet Shop Boys to create orchestral stabs in the mid-1980s.

Write your answer here (or complete on paper)...
Examiner Guidance: Fairlight CMI (or E-mu Emulator / Synclavier).
✍️ Task 4: Section B Extended 12-Mark Comparative Essay
Official 12-Mark Prompt

Evaluate the technological transition from early analog synth-pop (The Human League, 1981) to mid-80s digital sampling and MIDI production (New Order, 1983 / Tears for Fears, 1985). In your response, examine: • Hardware Sequencing & Synchronization • Analog Voltage vs. Digital Sampling • Gated Reverb & Mix Bus Processing.

Indicative Examiner Mark Scheme Content:

Contrast CV/Gate voltage sync and analog synth patch design against MIDI 5-pin DIN communication, digital FM bells, 8-bit/12-bit sample manipulation on the Emulator/Fairlight, and SSL console channel gating.
