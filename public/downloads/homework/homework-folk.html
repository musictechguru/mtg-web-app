🔍 Task 2: The 'Big 4' Specification Fingerprints

Memorize these core specification criteria across Equipment, Capture, Processing, and Soundstage:

1. Equipment & Instruments

Martin D-28, Gibson J-45 acoustic guitars, Appalachian dulcimer, banjo, upright double bass, acoustic upright piano, harmonicas.

2. Audio Capture & Micing

Stereo acoustic guitar micing configurations: XY coincident pair or Spaced Pair (A/B) pointing at 12th fret (avoiding the boomy soundhole). Small Diaphragm Condensers (Neumann KM184, AKG C451) for fast transient pick attack.

3. Processing & Effects

Extremely transparent signal chain. Gentle optical compression (Teletronix LA-2A) or vari-mu tube limiting (Fairchild) just shaving 1-2dB off peaks. High-pass filter at 80Hz to eliminate mic stand rumble. Natural room reverberation or subtle plate reverb.

4. Soundstage & Balance

Intimate, warm, organic stereo image. Acoustic guitars panned naturally or double-tracked and panned 9 o'clock / 3 o'clock; lead vocal intimate and dead center; subtle ambient room mics panned wide to recreate a physical performance room.

📝 Task 3: Section A Exam Practice Questions (10 Marks)

Answer the following Edexcel exam questions in full technical sentences using professional studio terminology:

Question 1 [3 Marks]

State the recommended microphone position when recording an acoustic steel-string guitar with a single condenser mic, and explain why.

Write your answer here (or complete on paper)...
Examiner Guidance: Aimed at the 12th fret (where the neck joins the body) approximately 6–12 inches away. This captures a balanced blend of body resonance and string brightness while avoiding the boomy low-end resonance of the soundhole.
Question 2 [3 Marks]

Describe the XY coincident stereo micing technique used on acoustic folk instruments.

Write your answer here (or complete on paper)...
Examiner Guidance: Two directional (cardioid) microphone capsules positioned as close as possible at an angle of 90° to 110° to one another, providing stereo imaging based purely on level differences with zero phase cancellation.
Question 3 [2 Marks]

Explain why Ken Caillat changed the strings on Lindsey Buckingham's acoustic guitar every single morning during the recording of Fleetwood Mac's 'Rumours'.

Write your answer here (or complete on paper)...
Examiner Guidance: To ensure maximum high-frequency harmonic brilliance and consistent transient snap across all multi-tracked fingerpicking takes.
Question 4 [2 Marks]

Identify two sonic hallmarks of Justin Vernon's lo-fi cabin recording aesthetic on Bon Iver's 'Skinny Love'.

Write your answer here (or complete on paper)...
Examiner Guidance: Natural room acoustics/reverberation, close dynamic mic proximity (Shure SM57), unpolished fret/finger noise, and audible preamp overdrive/saturation.

Compare the acoustic guitar recording and vocal production of Fleetwood Mac's 'Never Going Back Again' (1977) with Bon Iver's 'Skinny Love' (2007). In your essay, analyze: • Acoustic Guitar Transducer Selection & Placement • Studio Isolation vs. Ambient Room Bleed • Hi-Fi Commercial Production vs. Lo-Fi Intimate Authenticity.

Contrast Ken Caillat's multi-million dollar California studio, multi-mic Neumann condenser setup, pristine tape alignment, and surgical editing against Justin Vernon's isolated snowy cabin, single Shure SM57 microphone, unvarnished acoustic guitar rattle, and raw emotive vocal delivery.
