02 Technical Parameter & Circuit Architecture
Specification Parameter Engineering Description • Exam Definition
15–35ms Delay Window The optimal Haas doubling zone. Below 15ms causes comb flanging; above 35ms creates distinct slapback echo.
LFO Pitch Detune Low Frequency Oscillator modulates delay clock speed, continuously Doppler-shifting pitch sharp and flat.
Summing & Beating Combining unmodulated dry signal with modulated wet signal produces acoustic phase beating and richness.
Rate & Depth Rate controls LFO speed in Hertz; Depth controls amplitude of pitch detuning in cents.
Roland Dimension D Dual cross-mixed inverted delay lines creating dimensional stereo width with virtually zero audible pitch warble.
03 Section A: Specification Exam Questions (13 Marks)

Answer all questions in the spaces provided. Complete mark schemes and examiner guidance are included for self-assessment and peer marking.

Question 1 [3 MARKS]

State the typical delay time range used in a chorus effect and explain why this range is chosen.

Examiner Mark Scheme • Guidance: Award 1 mark for stating 15ms to 35ms. 1 mark for explaining that delays under 15ms produce comb filtering / flanging. 1 mark for explaining that delays over 35ms produce distinct slapback echoes; 15-35ms creates natural doubling.
Question 2 [3 MARKS]

Explain how an LFO modulating a delay line creates pitch variation in a chorus circuit.

Examiner Mark Scheme • Guidance: Award 1 mark for LFO modulating clock frequency/speed. 1 mark for Doppler effect: shortening delay time compresses audio wave cycles, raising pitch sharp. 1 mark for lengthening delay time stretching wave cycles, dropping pitch flat.
Question 3 [3 MARKS]

Describe how the Roland JC-120 amplifier produces true spatial chorus without summing signals electronically.

Examiner Mark Scheme • Guidance: Award 1 mark for two independent 60W power amplifiers and two 12-inch speakers. 1 mark for routing the dry untouched signal to one speaker and the pitch-vibrato signal to the other speaker. 1 mark for acoustic mixing occurring in the physical room air.
Question 4 [2 MARKS]

Why did Roland include built-in analog chorus circuits in polyphonic synthesizers like the Juno-6 and Juno-106?

Examiner Mark Scheme • Guidance: Award 1 mark for cost reduction: each voice had only a single DCO oscillator. 1 mark for built-in BBD chorus thickening and stereo-widening the sound to emulate dual-oscillator polyphonic synths.
Question 5 [2 MARKS]

Explain the mono compatibility risk associated with aggressive stereo chorus effects.

Examiner Mark Scheme • Guidance: Award 1 mark for recognizing that stereo chorus frequently phase-inverts the wet signal on one channel. 1 mark for explaining that summing to mono causes destructive phase cancellation, thinning or erasing the chorused sound.
04 Section B: Extended Evaluation Essay (12 Marks)

Extended 12-Mark Technical Evaluation Question

Analyze the technical operation, parameters, and artistic application of the chorus effect in modern music production. In your answer, refer to: • BBD analog delay lines and LFO modulation • Key parameters (Rate, Depth, Wet/Dry mix) • Differences between chorus, flanger, and vibrato • Musical application across guitar, synthesisers, and vocals.

Indicative Content • Examiner Commentary: • Physics & Circuitry: 15–35ms BBD delay window; LFO Doppler pitch detuning (Rate in Hz, Depth in cents). Combining dry + pitch-shifted wet = acoustic chorus beating.
• Comparison: Vibrato is 100% wet pitch modulation (no dry signal). Flanging uses 1-15ms with high feedback comb filtering. Chorus uses 15-35ms with zero/low feedback for smooth doubling.
• Dimension D & Stereo Processing: Dual delay lines with inverted LFO phase eliminate perceptible pitch wobbling while maximizing stereo width.
• Musical Applications: Kurt Cobain / Nirvana grunge guitars (EHX Small Clone); Andy Summers / The Police crystalline 80s pop tone (JC-120); Roland Juno synth pads; vocal thickening.
