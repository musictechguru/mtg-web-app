02 Technical Parameter & Circuit Architecture
Specification Parameter Engineering Description • Exam Definition
1–15ms Micro-Delay The ultra-short time window where delayed signal interferes acoustically with direct sound, creating comb filtering.
Harmonic Comb Series Linear series of peaks and troughs occurring at exact odd multiples: f = 1/(2*tau), 3/(2*tau), 5/(2*tau)...
Tape Flanging Origin Engineers physically pressed their thumb on the flange (outer rim) of a tape supply reel to induce micro-lag.
Positive vs. Negative Feedback Positive feedback reinforces odd harmonics (woody whoosh); negative feedback inverts wet phase, creating hollow metallic tubular bite.
Haas Precedence Limit Delays below 30ms are perceived as timbral alteration; delays above 35ms break into discrete slapback echoes.
03 Section A: Specification Exam Questions (13 Marks)

Question 1 [3 MARKS]

State the typical delay time range used by an audio flanger and explain what acoustic phenomenon occurs when it is blended with direct sound.

Examiner Mark Scheme • Guidance: Award 1 mark for 1ms to 15ms. 1 mark for destructive and constructive phase interference. 1 mark for stating that it generates a harmonically related comb filter series across the spectrum.
Question 2 [3 MARKS]

Describe the historical recording studio origin that gave 'flanging' its name.

Examiner Mark Scheme • Guidance: Award 1 mark for running two tape machines playing identical audio in synchronization. 1 mark for the engineer physically pressing a finger/thumb onto the metal flange (outer rim) of one tape reel. 1 mark for friction slowing that tape down by milliseconds, creating sweeping phase cancellation.
Question 3 [2 MARKS]

State the mathematical formula for the cancellation notch frequencies in a flanger comb filter where delay time is tau (seconds).

Examiner Mark Scheme • Guidance: Award 2 marks for f = (2n + 1) / (2 * tau) or specifying f = 1/(2*tau), 3/(2*tau), 5/(2*tau)... (Award 1 mark for mentioning odd half-wavelength multiples).
Question 4 [3 MARKS]

Explain how high Feedback (Regeneration) produces the iconic 'jet-plane whoosh' associated with flanging.

Examiner Mark Scheme • Guidance: Award 1 mark for recirculating the delayed signal back into the flanger input. 1 mark for resonance building up at the peaks between the comb notches. 1 mark for the LFO sweeping these resonant whistling overtones up and down the spectrum.
Question 5 [2 MARKS]

What is 'Through-Zero Flanging' (TZF) and why could it originally only be achieved using tape decks?

Examiner Mark Scheme • Guidance: Award 1 mark for explaining that the delayed signal passes through the zero-point and momentarily leads ahead of the reference signal, resulting in total phase cancellation silence. 1 mark for noting electronic pedals cannot achieve negative time delay.
04 Section B: Extended Evaluation Essay (12 Marks)

Extended 12-Mark Technical Evaluation Question

Evaluate the acoustic theory, technological development, and musical application of flanging in sound recording. In your answer, refer to: • Tape flanging origins vs. modern analog BBD circuits • Mathematical principles of comb filtering and notch spacing • Positive vs. negative feedback characteristics • Landmark listening examples (The Small Faces / Heart / Van Halen).

Indicative Content • Examiner Commentary: • Physical Origins: 1960s Abbey Road & Olympic Studios (Ken Townsend / George Chkiantz). Physical finger pressure on tape reel flange slows tape; releasing allows catch up.
• Mathematical Comb Filtering: 1–15ms delay creates linear harmonic notches at odd multiples f = (2n+1)/(2*tau). Swept across spectrum by LFO.
• Feedback & Polarity: Positive feedback accentuates even harmonics (deep warm jet sweep); Negative feedback inverts wet phase, creating hollow, metallic, tubular bite.
• Through-Zero Flanging: Unique tape phenomenon where tapes cross over, causing momentary absolute cancellation silence.
• Landmark Recordings: The Small Faces 'Itchycoo Park' (first psychedelic tape flange); Heart 'Barracuda' (galloping rhythm guitar comb filter); Van Halen 'Unchained' (MXR Flanger).
