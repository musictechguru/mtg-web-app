🔍 Task 2: The 'Big 4' Specification Fingerprints

Memorize these core specification criteria across Equipment, Capture, Processing, and Soundstage:

1. Equipment & Instruments

DAWs (Pro Tools, Logic Pro X, Ableton Live), Antares Auto-Tune Pro, Celemony Melodyne, Spectrasonics Omnisphere, Serum softsynth, pristine modern outboard (Sony C-800G, Neve 1073, Tube-Tech CL 1B).

2. Audio Capture & Micing

Ultra-clean vocal booth tracking. Intensive vocal comping (piecing together a master vocal track from 30+ takes). Close-mic pop shield configuration to control plosives.

3. Processing & Effects

Microscopic vocal pitch correction (Melodyne note-by-note tuning) and timing alignment (VocALign). Multi-band compression (FabFilter Pro-MB), parallel vocal compression, dynamic sidechain ducking, stereo widening plugins.

4. Soundstage & Balance

Hyper-wide commercial master. Mid-Side (M/S) stereo processing: lead vocal, kick, and sub-bass perfectly mono down the center; vocal harmonies, synths, and stereo reverb tails pushed 100% into the stereo sides.

📝 Task 3: Section A Exam Practice Questions (10 Marks)

Answer the following Edexcel exam questions in full technical sentences using professional studio terminology:

Question 1 [2 Marks]

Describe the function of VocALign software in commercial pop vocal production.

Write your answer here (or complete on paper)...
Examiner Guidance: Automatically analyzes the timing profile of a guide lead vocal track and phase-aligns backing vocal stacks to match the timing perfectly.
Question 2 [3 Marks]

Explain the difference between automatic pitch correction (e.g. Auto-Tune live) and manual graphical pitch correction (e.g. Melodyne).

Write your answer here (or complete on paper)...
Examiner Guidance: Auto-Tune instantly pulls incoming pitch to the nearest scale degree with configurable attack speed (often producing robotic transitions); Melodyne displays audio as pitch blobs in a visual grid, allowing manual adjustment of pitch, vibrato, drift, and formants without audible artifacts.
Question 3 [2 Marks]

State what 'vocal comping' means in modern DAW recording.

Write your answer here (or complete on paper)...
Examiner Guidance: Recording multiple takes (playlists) of a vocal performance and editing together the best words, phrases, or syllables into a single flawless master take.
Question 4 [3 Marks]

Describe how modern pop mixers use Mid-Side (M/S) processing on the master stereo bus.

Write your answer here (or complete on paper)...
Examiner Guidance: Decoupling the Mid (mono center signal) from the Side (stereo difference signal) to apply EQ and compression independently: keeping low-end punchy and mono in the Mid while brightening and widening stereo reverb and synths in the Sides.

Examine: • Vocal Comping, Pitch Correction & Layering • Rhythm Section Engineering (Analog Stems vs. Hybrid ITB) • Stereophonic Balance & Loudness Mastering.
