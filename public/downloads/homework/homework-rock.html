🔍 Task 2: The 'Big 4' Specification Fingerprints

Memorize these core specification criteria across Equipment, Capture, Processing, and Soundstage:

1. Equipment & Instruments

Fender Stratocaster, Gibson Les Paul, Marshall Plexi 100W heads, Vox AC30, Ludwig drum kits, Hammond B3 organ with Leslie 122 rotary speaker, Mellotron M400 tape-replay keyboard, EMS VCS3 synth.

2. Audio Capture & Micing

Multitrack tape (4-track moving to 8-track and 16-track). Close miking on drum heads combined with ambient room mics (Glyn Johns method or stone hallway capture). Ribbon mics on guitar cabs and drum overheads.

3. Processing & Effects

Artificial Double Tracking (ADT), tape flanging, tape phase shifting, backward tape reversal, plate reverb (EMT 140), tape tape delays (Echoplex, Binson Echorec), vintage fuzz pedals (Fuzz Face, Tone Bender).

4. Soundstage & Balance

Early stereo: disjointed ping-pong panning (drums hard left, vocals right). By 1973: fully balanced panoramic stereo soundstage with centered kick, snare, and bass, and wide double-tracked guitars and keyboards.

📝 Task 3: Section A Exam Practice Questions (10 Marks)

Answer the following Edexcel exam questions in full technical sentences using professional studio terminology:

Question 1 [3 Marks]

Explain how Ken Townsend invented Artificial Double Tracking (ADT) at Abbey Road Studios in 1966.

Write your answer here (or complete on paper)...
Examiner Guidance: Took sync playback head signal from master tape machine, routed to second tape machine with variable speed oscillator (varispeed), and combined with direct signal to simulate double tracking.
Question 2 [3 Marks]

Identify the acoustic space and microphone placement used to achieve the iconic drum sound on Led Zeppelin's 'When the Levee Breaks'.

Write your answer here (or complete on paper)...
Examiner Guidance: Three-story stone stairwell at Headley Grange; two Beyerdynamic M160 ribbon mics hung from banister above kit, compressed through Helios console limiter and Binson Echorec delay.
Question 3 [2 Marks]

Describe how tape varispeed was creatively utilized during the recording of 1960s psychedelic rock tracks.

Write your answer here (or complete on paper)...
Examiner Guidance: Recording an instrument or vocal at a lower or higher tape speed, so playback at normal speed alters pitch, timbre, and formant characteristics.
Question 4 [2 Marks]

State two technological differences between the EMT 140 plate reverb and a 1950s tape slapback echo.

Write your answer here (or complete on paper)...
Examiner Guidance: EMT 140 uses steel plate suspended on springs with transducer and pickups to simulate diffuse room decay (1-5s); tape slapback uses physical delay between magnetic tape heads with distinct single repeat.

Official 12-Mark Prompt
