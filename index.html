Edexcel A-Level Music Technology Revision FAQ

What does Component 3: Listening and Analysing cover?

Component 3 tests students' listening and analytical skills across commercial music recording history from 1950 to the present day. Key topics include recording techniques, acoustic principles, microphone placement, effects, and synthesis.

What does Component 4: Producing and Analysing cover?

Component 4 examines practical DAW skills, editing, mixing, signal flow, dynamic processing, corrective EQ, MIDI programming, and mastering considerations under exam conditions.

What revision tools does Music Tech Guru provide?

Interactive signal-chain builders, parameter matching drills, frequency recognition exercises, Component 3 & 4 full mock exams, a 3,300+ entry music technology dictionary, and teacher progress analytics.
